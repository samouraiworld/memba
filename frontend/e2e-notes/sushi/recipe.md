# Avocado & cucumber maki

> A small recipe for trying public Notes together.

## Ingredients — four rolls

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Sushi rice | 300 g |
| Water | Follow the rice packet |
| Rice vinegar | 45 ml |
| Sugar | 15 g |
| Salt | 5 g |
| Nori sheets | 4 |
| Avocado | 1 |
| Cucumber | 1 small |
| Soy sauce and pickled ginger | To serve |

## Make the rolls

1. **Rinse the rice**, then cook it following the packet instructions.
2. Mix the vinegar, sugar and salt. Fold the mixture gently into the cooked rice.
3. Cut the avocado and cucumber into thin strips.
4. Put a nori sheet shiny side down on a rolling mat. Spread a thin layer of rice over it, leaving a small strip uncovered at the top.
5. Add the vegetables across the middle and roll firmly. Moisten the uncovered edge to close the roll.
6. Cut into bite-sized pieces with a wet, sharp knife and serve.
